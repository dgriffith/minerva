Proposals

The Proposals panel is where the LLM proposes, the human confirms — the most important design decision in Minerva — becomes a screen you actually look at. Every graph write an AI operation wants to make waits here as a reviewable card until you approve or reject it; nothing lands in .minerva/graph.ttl on the strength of a model's say-so alone.

What shows up here

A proposal is a thought:Proposal node the approval engine (src/main/llm/approval.ts) creates for every graph change an LLM proposes — new claims, evidence links, component creation, note refactors/deletes, in-place note rewrites, and proposed source properties all file the same way. Each card bundles one or more payloads — a new note, a block of graph triples, or both — that apply atomically: approve lands the whole bundle, reject discards the whole bundle, never half of one.

Not scoped to the open note

Unlike every other panel in the right sidebar, Proposals is not filtered to the file you currently have open — ProposalsPanel.svelte is handed only a revision counter to know when to refresh, not an activeFilePath. It lists every proposal in the project regardless of which note is active, with a status-tab filter (All / Pending / Approved / Rejected) defaulting to All so the panel doubles as an audit trail — some everyday AI operations (auto-tag, auto-link) file a proposal and then self-approve it once you've accepted the suggestion on the conversation card, so they land under Approved rather than Pending. A panel that only ever showed "Pending" would hide most of what the AI has actually done.

Reviewing a proposal

Click a card to expand it. Each payload is a separate collapsible row — click one to preview its actual content: a note's full text, or the raw Turtle for a graph-triples payload. This is the diff view: you're reading exactly what will be written, not a paraphrase of it, before you decide.

Approve — y
Applies the proposal's full payload bundle via the approval engine's approveProposal() and flips its status to approved. The keystroke only fires while a card is expanded (selected) — handleKeydown checks selectedUri first. A success banner reports exactly what landed (note paths included, up to three, then "+N more").
Reject — n
Calls rejectProposal(), which flips status to rejected and applies nothing. The proposal node stays in the graph as a rejected record — rejecting doesn't delete it, so the audit trail shows what the AI suggested even when you turned it down.
Close — s / Esc
Collapses the expanded card without changing anything. Pure UI state, not a decision.
Status tabs
All / Pending / Approved / Rejected, filtering the list client-side by the proposal's stored status. Search and a Newest-first / By-type sort sit above the tabs for finding one proposal in a long history.

Every AI write lands here

There's no back channel: every graph change an LLM makes files a thought:Proposal, so this panel is a complete record of what the AI has touched. What differs is only where a proposal lands among the status tabs:

Held for review
New claims, evidence links, component creation, note refactors and deletes, note rewrites, source-property proposals. Filed as Pending and applied only when you approve — the everyday case.
Filed as approved
Operations you've already signed off on elsewhere — auto-tag and auto-link, where you accepted the suggestion on the conversation card — self-approve on the spot. They still write a full proposal, so they appear under Approved as a record of what happened rather than a decision to make.

Either way the proposal exists and is inspectable. Nothing the model writes to the graph bypasses this panel.
