Inspections

Inspections reviews the reasoning across your entire project — not just the note you have open — and flags weak spots: claims you've asserted without support, contradictions between things you've treated as settled, broken links, and messy sources. Think of it as a proofreader for your thinking. Every result is just a flag for your attention; Inspections never changes your notes on its own.

Coming in a future release

Inspections isn't available in the app yet — it's on the way. This page describes what it will do so you know what to expect when it arrives.

What it checks

Inspections looks for a handful of common problems in how your ideas are argued and how your sources are kept:

Unsupported claim
A claim you've stated with nothing backing it up — no evidence or reasoning behind it at all.
Missing warrant
A claim that has evidence behind it, but no explanation of why that evidence actually supports the claim.
Missing backing
A piece of reasoning that connects evidence to a claim, but with nothing to justify trusting that reasoning in the first place.
Contradiction
Two ideas you've both treated as settled that actually disagree with each other.
Source hygiene
Tidiness checks on your sources and links: sources missing details, duplicates, sources you cite but never read, and broken links or citations.

Running a check

Inspections refreshes on its own as you work, and you can force an immediate pass with the Run button in the panel. It only reads your project to report what it finds — it never edits anything.

Good to know

  • Clicking a result starts a conversation. Instead of jumping you to a spot in your notes, clicking a flag opens a new conversation about it, so you can talk it through with the AI and decide how to fix it.
  • Plain writing won't trip any flags. Inspections looks at claims, evidence, and reasoning. A note that's just ordinary prose has nothing for it to check, so it won't turn up any results.
